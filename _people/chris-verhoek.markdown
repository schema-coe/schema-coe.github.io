Chris Verhoek is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Electrical and Systems Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania, working with Nikolai Matni on control architecture theory. He received his PhD from Eindhoven University of Technology in 2025 with a thesis on data-driven control of nonlinear systems with stability and performance guarantees.
